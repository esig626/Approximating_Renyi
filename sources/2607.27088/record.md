## Relevance to this project

The transferable mechanism is a sum of conditional expected changes in a posterior quantity. The product assumption enters the control of future gain. Our plan isolates that assumption and investigates a replacement for dependent observation laws. Nonidentical marginals are already allowed by the motivating product setting.
